1. CFDs are high risk

CFDs are complex instruments and come with a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. Most retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs. You should consider whether you understand how CFDs work and whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money.

  • Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. A small price move against you can wipe out a large part of your margin.
  • Margin calls and close-outs: your provider may close positions automatically if your margin falls below its requirement, crystallising losses.
  • Gaps and slippage: prices can jump past your stop-loss, especially around news or market opens, so losses can exceed what you planned.
  • Costs: spreads, overnight funding (swap) charges and currency conversion all reduce returns.
  • Your provider publishes the percentage of its retail accounts that lose money. Check that figure on its website.

2. Risks specific to gold (XAU/USD)

Gold is sensitive to US interest-rate expectations, the US dollar, inflation data, central-bank activity and geopolitical events. It can move sharply and unpredictably, including outside UK hours. Liquidity can thin during holidays and around major releases, widening spreads.

3. Limits of AI and automation

  • AI models and agents can be wrong, can misread news, and can produce confident-sounding but incorrect analysis.
  • Data feeds can be delayed, incomplete or erroneous. Software, networks and third-party services can fail.
  • Guardrails such as blackout windows and loss limits reduce some risks. They do not eliminate them.
  • Market regimes change. Behaviour that held in the past may not hold in future.
