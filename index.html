The problem

Gold moves on news you can’t watch all day.

Central-bank speeches, US data releases, risk-off headlines. Gold reacts in seconds, and leverage magnifies every mistake. Most retail traders are reacting late, sizing by gut and trading through events they didn’t see coming.

Trading gold alone

  • Hours of chart and news watching to form one view
  • Surprise volatility around CPI, NFP and FOMC
  • Position sizes that ignore the stop distance
  • No written plan, so no way to review what went wrong
  • Generic “signal groups” with no reasoning or risk context

With GrokGold

  • Agents scan events, sentiment and structure continuously
  • Event-aware guardrails flag no-trade windows in advance
  • Every idea arrives with entry zone, invalidation and size guidance
  • A journal of every plan and its reasoning for review
  • Built for Trading 212 CFD users. You approve every order.
How it works

Specialist agents, one accountable decision.

Instead of a single black-box model, GrokGold splits the job into focused agents that check each other’s work. The proprietary weighting and prompts stay private. The process doesn’t.

Event & Sentiment Scanners

Track the economic calendar, central-bank commentary, headlines and market tone around gold and the US dollar.

Gold Intelligence

Turns raw inputs into context: trend, volatility regime, key levels and how gold behaved in similar conditions.

Decision Agent

Weighs the evidence and decides: long idea, short idea, or, often, stand aside. Every decision is written up with its reasoning.

Trading 212 Executor

Converts an approved plan into precise order guidance for your Trading 212 CFD account, with stop, target and size.

  • Max risk per idea
  • News blackout windows
  • Daily loss circuit-breaker
  • Full decision journal
  • Human approval by default

FAQ

Straight answers

Is this financial advice?

No. GrokGold is research software and educational content. Trade ideas are general and do not take your personal circumstances into account. You decide whether to place any trade. If you are unsure, speak to an FCA-authorised financial adviser.

Does GrokGold guarantee profits or publish win rates?

No. Nobody can guarantee profits from CFD trading, and most retail CFD accounts lose money. We don’t publish unverified win rates or “returns”. If we ever publish performance data, it will be clearly labelled, independently verifiable and shown with its limitations.

Will it place trades on my account automatically?

By default, no. The Executor produces precise order guidance and you approve and place every order yourself in Trading 212. We never ask for your Trading 212 password, and we don’t hold or move client money.

Are you connected to Trading 212?

No. GrokGold is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Trading 212. We design our guidance around how Trading 212 CFD orders work because that’s the platform our members use.

Why gold only?

Focus. Gold has its own drivers (real yields, the dollar, central-bank demand, risk sentiment) and a busy event calendar. Specialising lets the agents go deeper and lets us test guardrails properly.

What does the ProxyPi part actually do?

ProxyPi gives you a SOCKS5/HTTP proxy exiting from a Raspberry Pi on a real UK home broadband line. It’s useful for keeping research bots and tools on a stable, private UK residential IP instead of a shared data-centre address. It is not for bypassing broker rules, KYC, geographic restrictions or multi-account limits, and you must not use it that way.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are monthly and you can cancel at any time before your next billing date. See our terms for details, including your statutory cancellation rights as a UK consumer.

Who is this not for?

Anyone who can’t afford to lose the money they trade with, anyone under 18, and anyone looking for a “get rich quick” system. If that’s you, please don’t trade CFDs.
