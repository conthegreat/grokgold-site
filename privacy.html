Stars payments, if the Pro tier opens that way, are processed by Telegram. A Stripe Payment Link, if used, is processed by Stripe. We would receive a payment reference and the email on the Stripe payment, not the full card number.
  • Email you send us. The contents of a cancellation, complaint or privacy request.
  • Font choice. Your browser stores whether you allowed Google Fonts. See section 7.

7. Cookies, local storage and Google Fonts

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or social pixels. Do not add them unless the banner asks first and this notice is updated.

The storage we set is grokgold_consent in local storage. It remembers whether you allowed Google Fonts, for about six months, and it is used only for that choice.

These legal pages do not embed a font link. Your browser contacts Google only if you choose Allow fonts. Reject keeps your device’s fonts. Change your mind with .

The home page, the kit page, the newsletter page and the ProxyPi page currently include a Google Fonts link, so those pages contact Google when they load, before the banner runs. Rejecting removes the link for the next load and switches the page to system fonts. It does not undo a request already sent. Those links should be removed, or the font files self-hosted, so the choice is real on every page. [Marketing pages still preload Google Fonts.] Inter and JetBrains Mono are available under the SIL Open Font License. Check the licence files before self-hosting.
